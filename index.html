About

I'm an MBA candidate at Hanyang University, concentrating in marketing and international business, with a B.S. from the University of California, Riverside. I'm drawn to the place where customer insight meets commercial decisions: who a product is for, why they'd choose it, and what it should cost.

My toolkit is quantitative. I use segmentation, conjoint analysis, regression, and predictive modeling to find out what customers value, and consumer research to understand why. Before business school I spent time in wireless retail, where upselling on the sales floor taught me how buyers really make trade-offs. That instinct still shapes how I read a dataset.

I manage projects end to end, from framing the question and designing the research to presenting a recommendation. I work fluently in English and Korean. I'm now looking for product marketing and marketing analytics roles in the United States.
